/ on-topic

It knows your class

Your professor uploads the course materials. Scholr answers from those, so it uses the same definitions and methods you're being graded on.

/ anytime

Always open

Office hours end. Scholr doesn't. Ask the second you get stuck — late at night, the weekend before the exam, whenever.

/ clarity

Explains, not just answers

Ask follow-ups until it clicks. Scholr walks through the reasoning instead of handing you a number to copy.

Getting started

You need a join code.

Scholr works per course, so you'll get access through a class your professor sets up — usually a short join code they share. Once you're in, just start asking. If your professor isn't using Scholr yet, point them to the professor page.
